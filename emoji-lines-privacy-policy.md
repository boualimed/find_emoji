# Privacy Policy — Emoji Lines

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how **Emoji Lines** (the "App", "Game") handles information when you play it on your Android device. The App is published by **Bouali** ("I", "me", "the Developer"), an individual developer based in Tunisia.

By using the App you confirm that you have read this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

## 1. Summary

- I do not ask you to create an account, and I do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location.
- Your game progress and settings are stored **on your device** only.
- The App shows **optional rewarded video ads** through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect certain device and advertising data to serve and measure those ads, subject to your consent choices where required.
- The App is not designed for children (see section 8).

## 2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following data locally on your device so that the game works properly:

- Game progress (for example, unlocked levels and scores).
- In-game balances such as hints, and the number of rewarded ads watched today (used to apply a daily limit).
- Settings such as sound, music and theme preferences.

This data stays on your device. I do not receive it. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

## 3. Information collected through advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, to display rewarded video ads. You can choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game benefit (for example, extra hints, an undo, or skipping a level). Watching an ad is always optional.

When an ad is requested or shown, AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process, among other things:

- Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and similar device identifiers.
- IP address and approximate location derived from it.
- Device information (model, operating system version, language, screen and network information).
- App information and ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was loaded, viewed, completed or clicked).

This information is used by Google and its partners to deliver, measure, and secure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, where permitted, to personalise ads. I do not receive your advertising ID or a profile of you from this process; I only see aggregated reports in my AdMob account (such as impressions and estimated earnings).

For how Google uses data, see:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- AdMob and Google Play services data practices: <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>

## 4. Your consent and ad choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where consent is required, the App shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time you open it. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices. You may change your decision at any time from the **Privacy settings** option in the App (when shown).

On your Android device you can also:

- Reset or delete your advertising ID: *Settings → Privacy → Ads*.
- Opt out of ads personalisation: *Settings → Privacy → Ads*.

If you decline personalised ads, you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

## 5. Information I do not collect

I do not collect or sell:

- Your name, email, phone number, address, or contacts.
- Your precise (GPS) location.
- Photos, files, microphone or camera data.
- Payment information (the App has no in-app purchases at this time).

I do not sell your personal data.

## 6. Legal basis and your rights

I process the limited data described above on the basis of your consent (for ads, where required) and my legitimate interest in operating and improving a free game.

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Tunisian Organic Law No. 2004-63 on the protection of personal data), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Because I do not hold identifying data about you, most of these requests are handled directly through your device settings and the consent form described in section 4. For data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights through your Google account settings or Google's privacy tools.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Tunisia: the Instance Nationale de Protection des Données Personnelles, INPDP).

## 7. Data retention and security

Data stored on your device remains until you clear it or uninstall the App. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's own policies. I rely on the security protections of the Android platform and of Google's services; no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's privacy

Emoji Lines is intended for a general audience and is **not directed at children under 13** (or under the minimum age required by the law of your country). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact me and I will help resolve it.

## 9. International data transfers
